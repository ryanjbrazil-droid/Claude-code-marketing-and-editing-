What we check

Seven places money leaves a repair shop through the parts counter.

These are the categories we compare across your invoices, vendor statements, credit memos, return records and your own shop records.

Vendor profit-leak audit — categories checked
Ref Category What we're looking for
MC Missing vendor credits A credit you were promised or assumed, that never appeared on a statement.
CC Core credits Cores that went back but were never credited, credited short, or missed the return window.
RP Returned parts Parts returned to the vendor with no corresponding credit memo on file.
DC Duplicate charges The same invoice billed and paid more than once, often across two statement periods.
PX Pricing discrepancies Invoiced prices that don't match the quoted or agreed price on your account.
QX Quantity discrepancies Quantities invoiced that don't match what was ordered or what arrived.
UX Unmatched transactions Charges with no corresponding purchase order, work order, or repair order behind them.

How the audit works

Three steps, and the longest one is ours.

01

You pick one vendor

Whoever you spend the most with. Send a sample period — three months is plenty: invoices, statements, credit memos, and whatever your shop system can export for the same window. If some of it is hard to pull, send what's easy.

02

We reconcile it

We line your records up against the vendor's and work through all seven categories above, item by item.

03

You get the findings

A plain list of what doesn't line up, what it's worth, and what you'd need to send the vendor to ask for it back. It's yours to use, whether or not you ever talk to us again.
