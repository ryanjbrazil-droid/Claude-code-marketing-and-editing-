What we check

What we check — and pursue
Ref Category What we're looking for
MC Missing vendor credits A credit you were promised or assumed, that never appeared on a statement.
CC Core credits Cores that went back but were never credited, credited short, or missed the return window.
WC Warranty credits Warranty claims filed with the vendor or manufacturer that were never paid, or paid short.
RP Returned parts Parts returned to the vendor with no corresponding credit memo on file.

UX Unmatched transactions Charges with no corresponding purchase order, work order, or repair order behind them.

How it works

Four steps, and the long one is ours.

01

One vendor, one month

Pick whoever you spend the most with. We need four things for a single month: that vendor’s statement, their invoices, any credit memos they issued, and your return and core records. If your POs or repair orders are easy to export, those help — but they’re optional.

02

You get the findings first

A plain list of what doesn’t line up, what each item is worth, and the evidence behind it. Free. If we find nothing, we’ll tell you that. Nothing goes to your vendor at this stage.

03

You decide what we chase

You pick which items to pursue, if any. Two ways to run it: we build the claim and you send it — your vendor never hears from us — or we contact them directly on your behalf. Your call.

04

We chase it until it posts

We follow up on a schedule, escalate when it stalls, track what the vendor promises and when, and check the following statement to confirm the money or account credit actually landed. The case closes when it posts — not when someone says yes.

Your records

What we ask for, and what we never ask for.

We ask for four things, for one vendor, for one month: that vendor’s statement, their invoices, any credit memos they issued, and your return and core records. Your POs or repair orders help if they’re easy to export. That’s the whole list.

We never ask for bank access, any login or password, tax returns, payroll, customer lists, or anything from a vendor or month you didn’t pick.

You upload to a private folder only you and we can see — not email, because those statements carry your account numbers and pricing. We delete your records 30 days after we send your findings, and sooner if you ask. We never publish your name or your numbers anywhere. And we don’t contact your vendor about anything until you authorize it in writing.
