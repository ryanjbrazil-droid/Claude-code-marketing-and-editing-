### Where to Find Clients Fast (Do These This Week)

**1. Instagram DM Outreach — 20 per day**
Search hashtags like:
- `#smallbusiness` + your city
- `#localbusiness` + your city
- `#restaurantowner`, `#boutiqueowner`, `#realtor`, `#contractor`

Look for accounts with 200-5,000 followers that post inconsistently or have low engagement. DM them:

> "Hey [name], I noticed your [product/service] is really good but your social media isn't showing it off the way it deserves. I help small businesses like yours get consistent content and more eyeballs. I'm running a promo right now — free 15-min audit call, no pitch. Interested?"

**2. TikTok + Instagram Comments Strategy**
Go to any large creator in the small business / entrepreneurship space. Leave value-packed, specific comments on their recent posts. Example: "This is exactly why businesses need a content system — not just random posting." People WILL click your profile.

**3. Facebook Groups**
Join 5-10 local business Facebook groups. Post value content (tips, not pitches) 3x/week. When someone asks "anyone know a good social media person?" you're already known.

**4. Fiverr / Contra / Upwork Profile**
Set up a profile on at least ONE of these today. Even $200-$300 gigs add up fast and build reviews you can screenshot for social proof.

**5. Cold Email Local Businesses**
Find businesses in your area on Google Maps with a weak social presence. Email them:
> Subject: Quick question about [Business Name]'s social media

> "Hi [name], I looked up [Business Name] and noticed your Instagram hasn't been updated in a while. I help local businesses in [city] get consistent content and grow their following. Would you be open to a free 10-min call this week to see if I can help?"

## Track 3: TikTok + Instagram Content Machine

This is how you get views and inbound leads without paying for ads.

### The Content Formula (3-4 posts per week per platform)

**Type 1 — "Educational Hook" (60% of posts)**
Teach something valuable in under 60 seconds. These go viral.

Examples:
- "3 reasons your Instagram isn't growing (and how to fix it)"
- "I fixed this local restaurant's Instagram in 10 minutes — here's what I did"
- "The ONE thing killing your TikTok views"
- "How I'd redesign [popular local business]'s social media"
- "Small business owners: stop making this mistake"

**Type 2 — "Proof/Results" (20% of posts)**
Show client results, before/afters, screenshots of growth.
- "I took this account from 200 to 2,000 followers in 30 days — here's the breakdown"
- "Client DM reaction after their first viral reel"

**Type 3 — "Behind the Scenes" (10% of posts)**
You working, your process, your tools. Makes you relatable and builds trust.
- "Day in my life running a digital agency"
- "How I edit a client reel in 20 minutes"
- "My content creation setup"

**Type 4 — "Soft Sell" (10% of posts)**
Direct offer, but lead with value.
- "I offer free audits this week — drop AUDIT in the comments"
- "Link in bio if you want your social media handled"

## Track 4: Systems to Keep It Passive

Once you land clients and get traction, these systems keep money flowing without your constant attention.

### Tools to Set Up (Most Are Free)
- **CapCut** — Video editing, fast
- **Canva Pro** ($13/mo) — All graphics, templates, carousels
- **Later or Buffer** (free tier) — Schedule posts in advance
- **Stan Store** ($29/mo) — One link to sell all digital products
- **Notion** (free) — Track clients, content ideas, pipeline
- **Calendly** (free) — Automate booking for discovery calls
- **Stripe** — Get paid professionally

### Client Retainer System
1. Client pays via Stripe (monthly auto-charge)
2. You deliver content in batches (record/edit 1 week, batch deliver)
3. Use a content approval Google Doc — no back-and-forth DMs
4. Review call 1x/month, 15 minutes max

**Goal:** Each client should take 4-6 hours/month max once you're efficient.

## The #1 Rule

**Volume beats perfection.** Post imperfect content daily. Send 20 DMs even when you don't feel like it. The algorithm and the market both reward consistency over quality in the beginning. Get reps in, learn what works, then refine.

You don't need to go viral. You need 3 clients at $1,000/mo. That's $36K/year from 3 people. It's a very small number of yeses.
